# Encounter North Star — Beyond Minecraft: JetSetCraft's AAA Vision

> **OWNER-APPROVED TOP-LEVEL PRODUCT VISION · 2026-11-15 · ASPIRATIONAL, NOT SHIPPED.**
>
> **When a JetSetCraft gang encounter ignites, it must stop *feeling* like Minecraft. It must feel like the most breathtaking, exhilarating, ridiculously fun, dopamine-hitting skating, graffiti, music and rival-crew game imaginable — worthy of the very best of Jet Set Radio and HYPERFUNK, and ambitious enough to surpass those benchmarks with its own identity.**

## North-star promise

**An encounter is a temporary, fully interactive game-within-the-game, not "Minecraft mobs in costumes while a Spotify song plays."**

- **Instant fantasy:** a crew reveal can make a familiar road, rooftop, village square, canyon, railway or built arena *feel* like a bespoke, electrifying JetSetCraft stage through original art direction, staging, choreography, music, motion and meaningful gameplay. The source world remains real.
- **Your moves ARE the spectacle:** skilled skating, precise flow, original graffiti, dance, routes, tactical crew responses and combo peaks are the protagonist. Never cover up shallow interactions with camera tricks, sparkles, screen shake or obligatory cutscenes.
- **A high bigger than numbers:** earned surges of wonder, surprise, humor, swagger, meaningful risk, skill expression and victory should create the feeling the owner calls "dopamine hitting." Surprise should be memorable, not a repetitive reward machine, forced playtime or manipulative compulsion loop.
- **Competitive aspiration, independent originality:** benchmark the expressive style of **SEGA's Jet Set Radio** and the fast traversal, trick-powered boost, graffiti crew competition and online street style publicly described for **Team Reptile's HYPERFUNK**. **Outdo the *quality of the experience*; do not copy their characters, layouts, soundtrack recordings, logos, UI, tags, animations or protected assets.** Official references: [SEGA's Jet Set Radio description](https://www.segasammy.co.jp/cms/wp-content/uploads/pdf/en/ir/20231208_legacy_e.pdf), [HYPERFUNK official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3183480/HYPERFUNK/). Hyperfunk's listed plans are a reference, not tested release-state claims.

## The encounter transformation: eight beats, zero dead air

The following is a **proposed experiential production contract**, not fixed scripted timing or a claim of implementation:

| Phase | What the player should feel | Gameplay and implementation acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Signal** | "Something remarkable is happening here." | Original, crew-specific distant audio/animation/environment hints with no false alarm or forced interruption; visibility, audio cues, and consent |
| 2. **Reveal** | "Who ARE these legends?" | Distinct silhouettes, attitude, readable poses, original graffiti and music motif; energetic choreography never obscures hazards or steals controls |
| 3. **Stage ignition** | "This ordinary world just became a different game." | Reversible, terrain-fitted course, color/light and UI treatment; no destroyed buildings, invalid chunks, stolen inventories, overwritten vanilla screens or mandatory shaders |
| 4. **Movement lock-in** | "My skates, tricks and momentum feel unbelievably good." | Responsive riding, grinding, turn/landing/transfer precision, camera readability, all supported ride styles, predictable physics and choice of routes |
| 5. **Crew duel** | "They are alive, stylish, and pushing me to get better." | Original gang-specific mechanics, multi-character coordinated roles, reactive but fair rivals, on-foot AND skating dance, telegraphed counters, visible challenge scoring |
| 6. **Escalation** | "Every move is lifting the music and the crowd." | Layered reactive music, pose and crowd/rival reactions, lighting/environment states and combo variations triggered by *confirmed* actions; no guessed beat maps or stolen audio |
| 7. **Peak and earned payoff** | "I HAVE to see that again." | A spectacular but legible climax, synchronized crew/camera/announcer accents, authentic score/result, replayable spectacle with meaningful mastery; no fake wins, artificial input delay or unearned confetti |
| 8. **Encore and clean exit** | "That was a full game, and now my world is still mine." | Victory/defeat reactions, unlocks if approved, opt-in replay, resume world/controls/camera/audio, refund/release temporary resources, retain original world/mobs/provider state |

**The camera never becomes the boss.** Camera work may be cinematic, stylized, dynamic and optionally custom, but must not seize player control, conceal scoring/hazards, induce motion sickness, break third-party camera integrations or cut away from an essential trick.

## The quality pillars — all required; none can hide another's failure

1. **Movement beyond minigame quality.** Acceleration, top speed, boost, skating weight, powerslides, grinding, wall rides, parkour transitions, momentum conservation, air-to-ground and rail-to-ground transfers must be exhilarating *and* learnable. No teleport/physics desync masked with effects. All approved vehicles and movement compatibility remain first-class.
2. **Character animation with personality.** Authored crew entrances, posing, idle personality, rival reactions, emotional tells, hard-groove choreography, foot/hand contact, gear alignment and full body motion must read at full-speed and slow motion, not generic Minecraft locomotion with a hat. Character customization/CPM compatibility must be protected; high-quality unique animations need real visual QA.
3. **Music that *plays with you*.** Exact licensed/mastered tracks where rights permit, original identity themes, beat/phrase/stem-aware transitions when data exists, responsive combo accents and crowd/crew call-and-response. Immediate hit/landing feedback must be separate from musical quantization: **never delay player inputs or scores waiting for the beat**. Faction-specific routing, music volume controls, and a legal cleared-original fallback are non-negotiable.
4. **World becomes a stage.** Readable cel-shaded/ink-forward visual language, original street-art/typography, physically plausible camera, temporary signage, particles, crowd framing, reactive lighting, elevated rooftops, vertical line design and contextual biomes/structures. Preserve all source blocks and protected areas. Night-event rave spectacle should be jaw-dropping **without requiring photic overload**.
5. **Crews are iconic, not reskins.** Gabberina's mixes jumpstyle/gabber/hardtekk; Cumbias, Phonkistas, Hardtekkas, Bananettas, Signal '81, Honkistas, Footworkas, MoccaSins, mob gangs and each other approved crew need recognizable motion, music, art, quirks, strategies and surprise logic. One universal copied dance and one generic track do **not** pass.
6. **Rival AI that creates a story.** Crew tactics, authentic competitions, clever rivalry, control-driven ability changes, surprise intervention and readable defeat/recovery have to respond to the player. Rival success must be earned; do not inflate health, force stun-locks or cheat against input/hit detection to simulate intensity.
7. **Production-grade tactile polish.** Immediate UI feedback, tight hit/trick confirmation, distinct transitions and sound mix, earned combo peaks, beautiful and fast menus, satisfying restarts, optional expressive announcer and clean end-of-event resolution. Show actual play, not trailers pretending to be gameplay.
8. **Multiplayer, accessibility, performance and reversibility.** No special quality mode that silently abandons dedicated servers, modded worlds, variable hardware, motion/flash sensitivity, muted audio, large crews, or saved world state. Server owns scoring/awards; clients keep instant local feedback and reconcile safely. Meaningful graphics tiers keep *gameplay parity*, not artificially limited content.

## Non-negotiable engineering and ethics guardrails

- **Minecraft survives intact:** the installed mod remains compatible with vanilla/world/protection systems, mob ownership, inventories, blocks, mod providers and the user's personal builds. Session-owned decorations and arena overlays are temporary, isolated or fully cleaned. Do not permanently reskin unrelated areas or rewrite worldgen to make a cool video.
- **Real play, not forced spectacle:** core riding, combat, trick confirmation, Flow, score, camera consent, existing inputs, break/pause/leave and player agency take precedence over cutscene timing.
- **Original creations and authorized masters:** comparisons to Jet Set Radio, HYPERFUNK, Bomb Rush Cyberfunk, Killer Instinct, or any other title are creative benchmarks, not an asset license. Owner-linked Spotify tracks are selections, **not cleared game audio**.
- **Full game compatibility:** multiplayer reconciliation, mod interactions, 1.20.1 baseline and planned later versions, accessibility, GPU/CPU and frame pacing, server budgets, reload/rejoin and cleanup must pass on each affected build.
- **Do not claim completion from a concept:** check [Release Status](Release-Status), build artifacts, client captures, server test receipts, actual sound assets and real gameplay footage. Beautiful moodboards alone do not meet this requirement.

## Operational acceptance: prove the feeling in actual play

Use the detailed [**Encounter Experience North Star — Acceptance Scorecard**](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/blob/main/docs/ENCOUNTER_NORTH_STAR_ACCEPTANCE.md) for each production encounter. Each evaluation must include:

- **Player delight:** blinded/unprompted first-impression reports; did the scene feel like a cohesive premium action game rather than a Minecraft mod minigame? Did players spontaneously want another run, and why? Record both praise and exact failures. **Proposed** stretch benchmarks: median "standalone-game feel" and "spectacle earned by gameplay" ratings **at least 9/10** across multiple testers; actual thresholds need a representative playtest before final approval.
- **Move-to-music-to-image causality:** verify game inputs/confirmed actions visibly and audibly change the event, rather than a pre-rendered clip running independently. Log trace IDs and captured timings for input, action, score, audio and visual feedback. Compare to original music beatmaps only if legally supplied and measured.
- **Technical quality:** test actual 1% lows, p95/p99 frame times, input response, animation contact errors, missing media cases, GPU/CPU use, network jitter, server tick time, chunk protection, world cleanup and memory/resource lifecycle. Define a reproducible reference PC and modpack; a **60 FPS / 16.7 ms frame-time budget** is a provisional *starting benchmark*, not a measured result or a guarantee across all hardware.
- **Safety and recovery:** prove opt-out/decline, reduced motion/flash, mute, spectators, multiplayer disconnection/rejoin, failure, retry, save/load, overlay cleanup, world-state restoration, and rights-cleared fallback. No success claims without recorded evidence.
- **Competitive audit:** side-by-side *playable* movement, feeling, onboarding, expression, replay value and production finish against target reference games when accessible. Record specific wins/losses and iterate until neither a cooler trailer nor a mocked effect can pass as a gameplay-quality gain.

## Delivery ladder

1. **Playable signature vertical slice:** one human-crew or mob-crew rivalry in an existing safe terrain/arena, with real skating, coherent art, real crew animation, responsive reactions, original/cleared music and repeatable scoring.
2. **Signature music and performance integration:** exact beat metadata, synchronized but gameplay-responsive layers, original choreography and announcer/lighting systems without input delay, measured and tested in live sessions.
3. **World-to-show transformation:** environment overlays, cinematic-but-controllable camera, dynamic crowd/rival stages, procedural venue adaptation and immediate full cleanup on any exit.
4. **Cross-crew personality:** repeat the slice with a truly different gang, not a color swap; prove unique mechanics and personality and safe multi-rival arbitration.
5. **Content and platform scale:** maintain the bar across all approved crews, old and new Minecraft versions, ride styles, modpack providers, accessibility and dedicated servers.

**Release gate:** No encounter can be labelled “AAA-grade,” “Jet Set Radio/Hyperfunk-level,” “game-changing,” “fully transformed,” or “finished” on the strength of design documents, a song link or a screenshot. Only working native builds, repeatable real-game evidence, external playtesting and clean compatibility/safety regression results can earn the claim.
