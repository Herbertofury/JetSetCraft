# Modded Mob Gangs

[Mob Gangs](Mob-Gangs) · [Vanilla gangs](Vanilla-Mob-Gangs) · [Individual profile standard](Gang-Profile-and-Reference-Standard)

This catalog brings the existing authored creature gangs into the wiki's dedicated Mob Gangs section. **Every source mob has an individual record**, even when it belongs to a shared family. Preserve exact provider IDs and canonical gang names. Each record needs its own music/dance references, quirks, abilities/counterplay, challenge identity, art and unlocks under the profile standard. Unless explicitly linked, music/dance selections and finished art remain pending.

The [official Midnight project](https://modrinth.com/mod/the-midnight) describes a rewritten line with experimental 1.20.1 builds and different legacy versions. Its modern source is closed; classic source is not a reliable modern roster. Keep legacy and rewritten entity inventories separate.

- [ ] Give each present creature an individual gang identity, music/dance reference record, art brief, techniques and unlocks; preserve any existing authored names found during reconciliation.
- [ ] Separate hostile, passive, ambient, summoned/helper and boss actors; no fabricated legacy mobs or guessed entity IDs.
- [ ] Make the dark dimension's local materials, silhouettes and readable music-synced illumination part of its authored courses, without stealing source portal/AI behavior.
- [ ] Test provider absent/present, upgrade/removal, source-state restoration and multiplayer before calling support complete.
