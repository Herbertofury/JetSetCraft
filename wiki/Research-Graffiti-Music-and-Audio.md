## November 1 expansion

**60 additional tracks and exact editions · 58 distinct artist/collaboration credits · 8 verified 2026 releases · 16 cover cards · 10 official videos**

Checked **1 November 2026** against the first 100-track atlas and the current crew pages. This pass widens the city: sample collage and turntablism; Japanese jazz, funk and hip-hop; garage, jungle and liquid drum and bass; electro and breaking; game composers; and specific crew crossovers. There are **zero normalized title repeats** against the first 100 tracks, not merely zero repeated artist/title pairs.

The strongest new direction is to give JetSetCraft **more kinds of musical places**. A market, a dance circle, a quiet tagging wall, a late-night connector route and a crew confrontation should not all ask the soundtrack to perform the same job. These selections offer concrete ways to audition those differences.

**Status:** all 60 are new audition proposals, uncontacted, with permission not requested. Existing owner-selected tracks and previously reported grants keep their existing status. The canonical [music research page](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/wiki/Research-Graffiti-Music-and-Audio) and crew registers remain authoritative. No recording was downloaded or played during this research; the scene notes below are editorial proposals, not claims of a completed listening test or measured BPM, key, stems or loop boundaries.

### The complete 60-track expansion

Each entry gives a specific scene, a concrete audition, the selected edition and a public creator/label entry. Contact links are starting points for the user’s own outreach, not a claim that a booking address or storefront alone controls every right.

#### Sample collage, turntables and big beat — 14 selections

The largest missing shelf in the first pass. Explore music that can make a radio station, market, route or comic encounter feel populated by distinct personalities. C2C is the immediate movement/video crossover; RJD2 and Chinese Man are strong first auditions for everyday city life; The Avalanches and Kid Koala open the comedy lane.

##### 01. The Avalanches — [Frontier Psychiatrist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eS3AZ12xf6s)

**Scene to audition:** Honkistas: an absurd radio broadcast that spills into the street.

Audition sample collage as a cast of characters: a boombox can have a comic personality without adding another narrator. **Try this:** Storyboard a short Honkistas arrival with three absurd props, then test whether the selected video edition gives each reveal a distinct identity while enemy warnings stay readable.

**Exact edition:** Official music-video edition; album-length audio and instrumental availability are separate questions. [Creator / label entry](https://www.youtube.com/@AvalanchesOfficial).

##### 02. Beastie Boys — [Intergalactic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qORYO0atB6g)

**Scene to audition:** An oversized rival crew arrives in a miniature city.

A crew can sell itself through coordinated movement, scale and a single memorable sonic identity. **Try this:** Try a block-scale boss entrance with synchronized three-character poses; compare it with a free-form entrance to see whether coordinated posing better communicates the crew.

**Exact edition:** Official BeastieBoysVEVO video edition, linked from the verified artist channel. [Creator / label entry](https://beastieboys.com/discography/).

##### 03. DJ Shadow — [Organ Donor](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1660367631)

**Scene to audition:** A short rooftop score attack with one unmistakable musical motif.

Use a compact musical idea to make a route memorable; the player should remember the challenge after the HUD disappears. **Try this:** Build a small loop with one risky transfer and audition this recording through repeated attempts. Check whether restart fatigue appears sooner than route mastery.

**Exact edition:** Organ Donor on Endtroducing (Deluxe Edition); this link does not select Organ Donor (Extended Overhaul). [Creator / label entry](https://djshadow.com/).

##### 04. RJD2 — [Ghostwriter](https://officialrjd2.bandcamp.com/track/ghostwriter)

**Scene to audition:** The first time a player connects the whole neighbourhood into one route.

A city traversal theme should support discovery and achievement before the game adds combat stakes. **Try this:** Record the same route as a beginner and as an expert, then audition both against the track. Choose it only if the music supports the small success of learning as well as the full combo.

**Exact edition:** The artist’s Deadringer Bandcamp recording; the page’s 2021 date is not treated as the original recording date. [Creator / label entry](https://officialrjd2.bandcamp.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://officialrjd2.bandcamp.com/album/deadringer).

##### 05. Blockhead — [The Music Scene](https://blockheadnyc.bandcamp.com/track/the-music-scene)

**Scene to audition:** A mural alley that changes its visual logic as the player returns.

The track and its official animated video make a useful paired study of music, transformation and urban imagery. **Try this:** Design three visual states of the same alley, keeping collision unchanged. Audition a transition between states without camera cuts so the player retains their route map.

**Exact edition:** Artist Bandcamp album recording. Ninja Tune’s official video is supplied separately; edition release dates differ between storefronts. [Creator / label entry](https://blockheadnyc.bandcamp.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://blockheadnyc.bandcamp.com/album/the-music-scene). [Official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NhheiPTdZCw).

##### 06. Chinese Man — [I've Got That Tune](https://chinese-man.bandcamp.com/track/ive-got-that-tune)

**Scene to audition:** A market arcade where merchants, signs and rails share one rhythm.

Try a playful sample-based identity for ordinary city life, giving the world character outside crew confrontations. **Try this:** Audition a route through a market with vendors and signage; leave space between ambient voice barks so the recording and local characters do not compete.

**Exact edition:** The artist’s The Groove Sessions, Vol. 1 recording. Conflicting artist/label storefront dates are not used to infer an original release chronology. [Creator / label entry](https://chinese-man.bandcamp.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://chinese-man.bandcamp.com/album/the-groove-sessions-vol-1).

##### 07. The Go! Team — [Ladyflash](https://thegoteam.bandcamp.com/track/ladyflash)

**Scene to audition:** A daytime street-party takeover after finishing a neighbourhood.

Celebration can come from collective energy rather than a larger explosion or a louder victory sting. **Try this:** Stage a victory street party with skaters, dancers and bystanders on one route. Audition whether this recording makes the return trip feel newly welcoming.

**Exact edition:** Studio recording from Thunder, Lightning, Strike; not the separately released Roundhouse live edition. [Creator / label entry](https://thegoteam.bandcamp.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://thegoteam.bandcamp.com/album/thunder-lightning-strike).

##### 08. Fatboy Slim — [Right Here, Right Now](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ub747pprmJ8)

**Scene to audition:** A montage showing one district evolve through the player’s actions.

The official video is a clear starting point for studying change over time as a visual idea. **Try this:** Compare a linear completion montage with a sequence of in-world before/after views. Keep the player’s tags recognizable at every stage of the transformation.

**Exact edition:** Official 4K music-video edition; not the 2026 rework or an assumed album-length cut. [Creator / label entry](https://www.youtube.com/@FatboySlim).

##### 09. The Chemical Brothers — [Block Rockin' Beats](https://www.thechemicalbrothers.com/music-videos/block-rockin-beats-video)

**Scene to audition:** A freight-yard chase with large, readable industrial landmarks.

Test whether a strong central musical identity can carry a hectic route without requiring a new cue at every obstacle. **Try this:** Audition a freight route with only three landmark changes. Keep warning sounds fixed and evaluate whether the music makes the route feel forceful without hiding approaching hazards.

**Exact edition:** Official artist video page; music-video edit selected, not an unspecified remix. [Creator / label entry](https://www.thechemicalbrothers.com/).

##### 10. Propellerheads — [Spybreak! (Short One)](https://propellerheads.bandcamp.com/track/spybreak-short-one)

**Scene to audition:** A compact pursuit through a station concourse.

A short, explicitly named version is useful when comparing a focused event against a long free-roam session. **Try this:** Audition one self-contained chase with a clear escape point, then compare a clean end with a return to the district station. Do not tie success to the recording’s runtime.

**Exact edition:** Spybreak! (Short One) from the official single page; not Long One or the Decksandrumsandrockandroll album entry. [Creator / label entry](https://propellerheads.bandcamp.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://propellerheads.bandcamp.com/album/spybreak).

##### 11. C2C — [Down The Road](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpc3cCxWNkU)

**Scene to audition:** A showcase route where unusual tricks change how ordinary objects are read.

The official video explicitly features skater Richie Jackson: a particularly useful music-and-movement crossover reference. **Try this:** Author five different interactions with a single ordinary street object. Audition the route before adding extra geometry, checking whether expressive traversal itself supplies enough variety.

**Exact edition:** Official music video starring Richie Jackson; the selected reference is the video edition. [Creator / label entry](https://www.c2cmusic.fr/).

##### 12. Kid Koala — [Fender Bender](https://kidkoala.bandcamp.com/track/fender-bender)

**Scene to audition:** Honkistas: a physical comedy encounter assembled from tiny interactions.

Turntable storytelling suggests a way to make a comic encounter legible through sound and action together. **Try this:** Mock up a collision, recovery and boast as three separate animations. Audition the recording while testing whether the player can still identify the exact moment control returns.

**Exact edition:** Carpal Tunnel Syndrome studio recording on the artist’s page; not a live performance or later reinterpretation. [Creator / label entry](https://kidkoala.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://kidkoala.bandcamp.com/album/carpal-tunnel-syndrome).

##### 13. Cut Chemist — [The Garden Stereo Remaster Main Mix](https://cutchemist.bandcamp.com/track/the-garden-stereo-remaster-main-mix)

**Scene to audition:** A rooftop garden linking two very different parts of the city.

The artist describes sessions in Los Angeles and Brazil; use this as a prompt for intentional cross-city musical exchange. **Try this:** Audition the transition from a dense street to a planted roof, using the same movement vocabulary in both. Ask whether the new setting feels connected rather than like a disconnected bonus level.

**Exact edition:** The exact July 11, 2026 Stereo Remaster Main Mix; the artist page marks this release as subscriber material. It is not silently substituted for the earlier original. [Creator / label entry](https://cutchemist.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://cutchemist.bandcamp.com/album/the-garden-stereo-remaster-ep).

##### 14. RJD2 & Supastition — [Back Talk](https://officialrjd2.bandcamp.com/track/back-talk-2)

**Scene to audition:** A rival crew’s radio transmission before a scored challenge.

A contemporary producer/MC collaboration can refresh the radio palette without abandoning a hip-hop foundation. **Try this:** Audition the track against a rival challenge announcement. Move game dialogue out of vocal passages instead of making every line compete with the song.

**Exact edition:** Back Talk from According To..., the artist-listed February 27, 2026 RJD2 & Supastition album; album track URL ending -2. [Creator / label entry](https://officialrjd2.bandcamp.com/). [Release / supporting source](https://officialrjd2.bandcamp.com/album/according-to).


#### Japanese jazz, hip-hop and sound design — 10 selections

Broaden the Japanese reference beyond the obvious game lineage. There is room for live jazz, beat music, reflective hip-hop and deliberately designed electronic spaces. These are useful candidates for places where the player slows down, experiments or returns after mastering a route.

##### 15. Nujabes feat. Cise Starr & Akin — [Feather](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1078914485)

**Scene to audition:** A sunrise free-roam route after the city’s main danger has passed.

Give mastery an emotional reward: familiar streets can feel different when the game allows the player to linger. **Try this:** Audition a no-timer morning lap with minimal HUD. Compare how often players stop to look around versus continuing the same route at maximum speed.

**Exact edition:** Modal Soul recording featuring Cise Starr and Akin. This Apple listing spells Cise Star with one r; the artist name is retained as Cise Starr in the research credit. [Creator / label entry](https://starbase.jp/artists/nujabes/). [Release / supporting source](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1445812701).

##### 16. Nujabes — [aruarian dance](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1721843001)

**Scene to audition:** A secluded tagging spot with no encounter and no countdown.

A quiet place can become a destination in its own right when its music promises relief rather than another task. **Try this:** Place a paintable wall at a lookout reached through a short optional route. Audition while choosing colours and framing a screenshot, keeping the session free of interrupting objectives.

**Exact edition:** Samurai Champloo Music Record Departure recording, corroborated by the original FlyingDog catalogue page; no fan remix selected. [Creator / label entry](https://starbase.jp/artists/nujabes/). [Release / supporting source](https://www.jvcmusic.co.jp/flyingdog/-/Discography/A010090/VICL-61411.html).

##### 17. Uyama Hiroto — [Waltz for Life ~song for children~](https://rophrecordings.bandcamp.com/track/waltz-for-life-song-for-children-3)

### Five practical improvements suggested by the expanded listening shelf

#### 1. Give the same district several emotional uses

Prototype a district’s **discovery route**, **crew event** and **afterglow visit** with three audition candidates. For example, Ghostwriter for discovering the route, Egypt, Egypt for a boombox confrontation, and Feather for the sunrise return. The proposal is about occasions in a place, not automatic genre switching whenever a player crosses a chunk boundary. A district should remain recognizable when its music changes.

#### 2. Build a public dance space before adding another crew

The electro shelf and Floor Kids reference suggest a reusable court: spectators around the edge, a clear performance area, an entrance and an exit. Breaking, footwork and skating can use the same architecture while retaining different animations and scoring language. Begin with a few readable original moves and clear turn-taking. That provides a more useful audition than comparing dozens of tracks against an empty floor.

#### 3. Make radio personality visible in small props and behaviour

The Avalanches, Kid Koala and Mr. Oizo point toward three prototypes: a collage of strange radio characters, a short physical-comedy scene and a mascot with one excellent idle. Each can sit around a boombox or shop counter. Keep the routine small enough that players recognize it without a cutscene, and test repeated visits before expanding the joke.

#### 4. Use warm music to test whether movement is satisfying by itself

Try Rising Son, Even If or Airglow Fires against a demanding route. If the route only feels exciting with an aggressive cue and dense visual effects, examine its corners, recovery paths and landmark rhythm. Music should amplify confidence, not conceal weak movement. This is an audition question; no track has been tested in the mod yet.

#### 5. Treat edition identity as part of the future music data

The research uncovered several names that conceal different recordings: Spybreak! (Short One), the 2010 Peshay Rework, Clear’s Jose “Animal” Diaz Remix, the 2023 Messij remaster, the Ape Escape reconstruction, the Data Discs Go Straight selection, DJ Clent’s 2023 version, and Funk Fiction’s Green Hill Zone cover arrangement. Store a stable recording identifier, exact title, credited artists, source URL and version note alongside a future audition decision. Do not assume a familiar title uniquely identifies the intended audio.

#### Make the soundtrack participate in the game

The repository already documents **80 original 3.2-second gang entrance stingers**, stable gang IDs and data-driven music references. Those are concrete foundations. Full-track music integration, synchronized dance playback and provider behaviour should remain labelled implementation work until demonstrated. See the [boombox runtime contract](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/blob/main/docs/BOOMBOX_GANG_RUNTIME.md) and the [live music/dance specification](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/wiki/Music‐Driven-Dances-and-Mob‐Themed-Bosses).

##### 1. Give each district a small musical vocabulary

Choose a handful of compatible **roles**, not a random mountain of songs: ordinary exploration, heightened pursuit, social gathering, crew challenge, victory and cooldown. A neighborhood should be recognizable before a title card appears. Use the atlas to audition alternatives for those roles; do not force every crew into the same sound because its songs share a rough tempo.

A useful first prototype has one warm home district, one fast technical route and one dramatic rival encounter. That gives the music a chance to express contrast. Expanding to every crew before these three scenes work would make a huge library harder to judge.

##### 2. Preserve immediate player control and sound feedback

The owner’s Killer Instinct-level ambition is about music responding to combat and trick chains. Landings, jumps, hits, inputs and simulation must remain immediate. Short original accents can acknowledge actions immediately; longer arrangement changes can enter at an appropriate musical boundary. Do not delay a successful jump until the next beat or silently change physics to fit a song.

If an authorized recording arrives as one stereo file, treat it as one stereo file. Layering clean, original reaction sounds over it is a more credible first prototype than promising nonexistent stems. Where actual editable stems or alternate arrangements become available, use them to make a clearly audible difference between cruising, a growing combo and a major payoff.

##### 3. Keep one event timeline and one clear musical owner

A boombox event, a rival challenge and a Honkistas takeover need an explicit handoff. The preceding event ends; the rival owns its challenge cue; the next takeover receives ownership only at the appropriate event transition. Avoid overlapping full songs, duplicate late-join starts, or a victorious stinger accidentally retriggering the entire encounter.

Late-arriving players need the current event, cue identity and meaningful playback position. Where a source cannot seek reliably, use an agreed join point or an original fallback rather than pretending all clients are synchronized. Transitions should have defined cancellation and cleanup behaviour, including travel, disconnects and the event ending before a track does.

##### 4. Give the Honkistas takeover a recognizable musical idea

The strongest proposal is **recognition followed by transformation**. An original motif heard in the preceding encounter returns in a surprising clown-associated arrangement, then gives way to a distinct challenge score. The player understands what has been hijacked. The Clown Core and Igorrr links are useful studies in contrast; they are not a prescription to paste chaotic full songs over existing music.

A sudden edit should still preserve fair hazard cues and readable animation. Visual choreography, music, announcer payoff and temporary lighting should refer to the same event progression. A surprise can be funny and threatening without making the game hard to parse.

##### 5. Keep music packs convenient and credits visible

Extend the current stable identities with optional, explicit music-pack definitions. A reviewable record would identify the exact track/version, local asset or supported provider, public credit link, any validated cue markers, and a known fallback. “Original Mix,” “Live,” “Extended Version,” “Instrumental,” and “Remix” are meaningful distinctions, not decorative suffixes.

Keep external audio loading out of world startup and keep a clear now-playing credit near the boombox/music interface. If a pack is missing, the original stinger and base event should still make sense. Loudness should be evaluated in the actual game mix, with music, announcer, crew voice and skating sound independently controllable. Muted play must still communicate the event and its payoff visually.

##### 6. Ask for useful deliverables when a track wins the audition

The next useful artist conversation is concrete: the exact recording, the scene it supports, whether a full track or a bespoke cue is wanted, and whether stems, instrumental, clean edit, loopable sections or a short victory ending actually exist. The public artist/label entry URL is retained for every candidate in the JSON; no contacts have been guessed and nobody has been messaged.

This also makes a commission brief much better: “a recognizable crew hook, an exploration arrangement, an escalation arrangement and a short original payoff” gives an artist a game design problem to solve. Avoid turning the reference into an instruction to copy another artist’s melody or voice.

#### The audition that would settle the first soundtrack choices

Use the same three short gameplay captures for each of the 16 starting candidates: a free-skating line, an encounter with a readable combo escalation, and a quiet social space. Judge each recording on four practical questions: does it give the scene an identity; does it help the player feel the movement; can important sound effects and dialogue still be understood; and does the cue remain enjoyable on repeated attempts?

Record an exact recording/version, the intended role, any actual timestamps discovered during listening, and the reason a candidate wins or loses. Leave tempo, key, phrase markers and usable loop points blank until they have been checked. That produces a small, defensible first selection while keeping the other discoveries ready for future districts and crews.

#### Source and handoff files

- `music-catalogue.json`: all 100 candidates, exact links, categories, proposed roles, version notes, status, 12 observed cover URLs and six video destinations.
- `music-page-metadata.json`: publisher HTML metadata from 34 album/release pages; one KOAN Sound page has an explicit fetch error, so its selected track was verified separately through web retrieval.
- The original wiki pages remain the authority for owner-selected tracks, aliases and sequence requirements. This atlas adds research, not approvals or shipped-feature claims.

**Artist discovery is deliberately broad.** Famous recordings, independent producers, current scene releases and game composers all remain eligible for the owner’s personal outreach; the shortlist has not been filtered by assumed licensing availability.
