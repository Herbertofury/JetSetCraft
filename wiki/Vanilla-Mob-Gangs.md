# Vanilla Mob Gangs

[Mob Gangs](Mob-Gangs) · [Profile requirements](Gang-Profile-and-Reference-Standard) · [Modded gangs](Modded-Mob-Gangs)

Approved names and authored hooks recovered from the existing [master atlas](Gang-Wars-Boombox-and-Mob-Atlas). This is a design/identity catalog, not a claim that every ability, reward or dance is implemented. Preserve stable gang IDs when names change.

Individual music and dance references and finished art are pending unless explicitly attached to a profile. Existing original entrance stingers do not substitute for the requested full music/dance reference treatment. All rows require the same individual profile fields; shared families do not erase per-mob identity.

The original atlas lists Spider in both conditional and hostile contexts; this is one entity/crew, not two collectibles. Warden and Elder Guardian likewise cross-link to their special-encounter treatment. The listed disposition is design lineage, not permission to rewrite source AI; Pufferfish remains a defensive source creature despite the historical hostile-section placement. Technical entities stay opt-in.

## Flagship profiles: existing direction to develop

### Creepaku Gouji — Creeper
- Personality/quirk: reckless explosive speed specialists, sudden bursts and risky close passes.
- Signature challenge: blast-launch trick lines and aggressive paint pushes; keep event explosions terrain-safe.
- Technique/unlock direction: explosive boost gear and volatile momentum tricks. Exact unlock thresholds and numeric tuning remain pending.
- Dance brief: emphatic build-up, readable release and recoil/recovery motifs that work on-foot and while skating. This is an authored choreography direction; individual video/music references are still needed.
- Counterplay to specify: readable blast cue and escape route, interruption/failure rules, recovery window, and no hidden damage to builds.

### The Bone Drones — Skeleton
- Personality/quirk: unsettling precision and synchronized rail formations.
- Signature challenge: technical line accuracy, ranged paint pressure and coordinated transfers.
- Technique/unlock direction: precision landings, rail control and ranged graffiti accuracy.
- Dance brief: sharply synchronized angular poses and rail-to-formation transitions; exact references pending.
- Counterplay to specify: line-of-sight breaks, shot/paint tells and fair accuracy limits without omniscience.

### Arachnaphobia — Spider
- Personality/quirk: vertical ambush specialists; keep source day/night nuance outside events.
- Signature challenge: wall-to-wall routes, vertical grinding, leap transfers and readable web hazards.
- Technique/unlock direction: Spider Skates with wall skating/adhesion and vertical transfers; ceiling interaction requires proven collision and camera safety.
- Dance brief: multi-leg contact rhythms, body-level changes and wall-to-ground transitions rather than forcing a human two-foot rig; exact references pending.
- Counterplay to specify: telegraphed leap/web placement, alternate routes and no unavoidable trapping.

### Dead Beat — Zombie
- Personality/quirk: appears shambling until the beat drops, then locks into coordinated rhythm.
- Signature challenge: endurance trick chains, visible dance battles and coordinated territory pushes.
- Technique/unlock direction: rhythm/combo equipment and dance-chain rewards; Junior identity Dead Beep.
- Dance brief: loose introductory movement resolving into tight call-and-response crew synchronization; exact references pending.
- Counterplay to specify: readable formation openings and endurance pressure, without unlimited participants becoming a per-tick global scan.
