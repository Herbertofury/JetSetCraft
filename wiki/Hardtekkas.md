# Hardtekkas: Hardtekk Rival Gang

**Status: owner-approved name and requested fourth rival; design and actionable backlog only. No gameplay implementation or runtime verification is claimed.** Recorded October 12, 2026 UTC.

**Tracking:** [Hardtekkas issue #31](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/issues/31) · [Shared music/choreography issue #30](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/issues/30).

## Explicit owner direction

- Create a separate hardtekk gang named **Hardtekkas**, alongside Gabberina's, Cumbias and Phonkistas.
- **Tekkistas** is an optional name unlocked once the player reaches the appropriate reputation/good-standing level. It is not an automatic or irreversible faction replacement.
- Begin the music reference collection with Drucktherapie and Wake Up below; the owner subsequently added Break It Down and subsequent references, retained below.
- Carry the established major requirements into this crew: exceptional music-generated/driven dance variety, distinctive skating and on-foot performance, ownership-aware mob-themed bosses, and shared player/enemy solo-to-crew and rival head-to-head choreography.

The new faction does **not** remove hardtekk from Gabberina's. Their explicit jumpstyle + gabber + hardtekk blend, **Hardtekk Tutorial**, and all earlier music/video references remain part of their design.

## Reputation and optional Tekkistas name

Use the existing [Crew Naming Rights design](Gang-Wars-Boombox-and-Mob-Atlas#52-player-unlock-crew-naming-rights) and [canonical gang reputation](Hangouts-Territory-and-Reputation#canonical-gang-reputation), rather than creating a separate grind or per-NPC reputation counter.

The current master specification already says that the configured **Friendly** reputation threshold unlocks Crew Naming Rights in the Gang Atlas. Its durable relationship is player UUID + stable gang ID; names are presentation metadata. Hardtekkas is the canonical default, and Tekkistas is the owner-approved optional unlocked name within that system.

Existing naming safeguards apply:
- Keep the same stable faction identity, reputation, membership, control, powers, music, boss selection, rewards, Atlas discovery, Boombox targeting and campaign defeat records.
- Let the player see and restore the canonical Hardtekkas name.
- Multiplayer defaults to personal aliases; shared world aliases require the server's existing permission/vote policy. Server canonical-name locks and resets remain respected.
- Choosing Tekkistas must not create a second faction, restart progression, grant duplicate rewards, revive a defeated rival or make one player's cosmetic choice rewrite everyone else's state.

**Unresolved:** no numeric Friendly threshold is defined here. Confirm whether Tekkistas is a suggested preset within general Crew Naming Rights or a separate themed unlock, whether any extra rank is intended, and how eligibility behaves after reputation loss, betrayal/reconciliation or server alias-policy changes. Do not silently invent these rules. Proposed acceptance: the choice is deliberate and resettable through the existing alias flow.

## Proposed creative direction to research and validate

The following are design proposals, not additional owner-approved roster or mechanics:
- Give the Hardtekkas their own original hardtekk-centered sonic identity, silhouettes, fashion, movement vocabulary and challenge personalities. Exact roster, genders, palette, outfits, substyles, names and final music remain open.
- Explore forceful, tightly timed skate/dance phrases, complementary crew roles, dynamic formation exchanges and escalating call-and-response. Derive actual footwork and timing from direct music/movement research, not track titles.
- Plan anticipation → recognizable musical entrance → playable challenge → escalation → memorable finale. Carry musicality and variety through repeated gameplay, rather than relying on one cinematic.
- Distinguish them from Gabberina's broader jumpstyle/gabber/hardtekk blend through deliberate composition and choreography. Shared genre influence does not justify copying the other crew's routine with a recolor.
- Keep mastery, timing, route choice and readable counters central. Avoid unavoidable reveal damage, permanent stun-locks or effect spam.

## Shared four-rival campaign integration

Extend the [multi-rival campaign](Cumbias‐and‐Multi‐Rival‐Gang‐War) to the player plus Gabberina's, Cumbias, Phonkistas and Hardtekkas:
1. Track source mob-gang influence/control and borrowed powers independently for each rival. Define partial influence, contested control, full takeover and rival-to-rival changes explicitly.
2. **Full takeover removes the corresponding source-gang power from the affected rival.** Aliases, boss cosmetics, reactive music and dances must never restore stripped abilities. Resolve recapture/restoration and already-started abilities deterministically, with visible player feedback.
3. After a normal mob event resolves, a rival retaining qualifying control may stage a separate surprise challenge. Record the original result/rewards first; select/stage eligible rivals through one clear arbitration rule, without competing music, duplicate callbacks, recursive encores or stale ownership checks.
4. Preserve independent persistent rival defeats and a clear overall campaign conclusion. Defeating or renaming Hardtekkas must not change another rival's progress. Optional rematches must not erase wins or silently revive a hostile campaign.
5. Preserve source mobs, UUIDs, inventories, ownership, provider data, AI recovery, optional-mod fallbacks and existing movement/combat authority. Never replace provider-owned mobs to implement faction powers.

Exact influence sharing, takeover thresholds, encounter frequency, ability mapping, rewards, defeat conditions and restoration rules remain shared design work.

## Music, choreography and ownership-aware bosses

Use the [canonical music-driven dance and boss specification](Music‐Driven-Dances-and-Mob‐Themed-Bosses) and [shared issue #30](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/issues/30).

- Hardtekkas need exceptional signature dances and meaningful phrase/transition/formation variety, including mob-specific anatomy/rig adaptations. A generic loop or tempo-only reskin does not satisfy the quality target.
- Players and enemies can perform solo, let teammates join, and face rival crews head-to-head, both on foot and while skating. Design deliberate join/leave, leader handoff, complementary roles, safe moving formations, mode transitions, interruption recovery and graceful solo fallback.
- For each supported Hardtekkas-controlled mob gang, design a distinct **mob-themed human boss** combining the controlling faction's music/style and source mob's theme. Preserve the underlying mob gang. No final Hardtekkas boss roster is approved yet; Hakken Horse remains the owner-specified Gabberina/horse example.
- Boss selection and significant choreography follow current authoritative ownership, with explicit neutral/player/contested/recapture behavior and safe in-flight transitions.
- Apply the owner's major **Killer Instinct-inspired reactive music quality goal**: fights, confirmed hits, skate trick chains/landings and combo escalation should drive musical accents/layers and memorable announcer-style payoffs. See the canonical specification's reactive-music section. This is an inspiration target, not a claim about Killer Instinct's internals or permission to copy its recordings.
- Never delay player input, hits, tricks or landings to fit a musical grid. Proposed phrase-aware structural transitions coexist with immediate event feedback. Keep crew choreography, rival music handoffs and multiplayer timing coherent.
