### Existing identities whose treatment must change
- **Zombie Horse / Night Mares** already exists in the old atlas. Mounts of Mayhem makes it relevant to ordinary newer-version mounted encounters; do not leave a universal “technical-only” restriction on all providers or create a duplicate gang. Preserve the old-version availability gate.
- **Tiny Takeover** changes baby designs and life-stage presentation. Update the Junior Atlas and rigs, including each applicable variant; do not count remodeled babies as dozens of new species. [Official release](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/play-the-tiny-takeover-drop).
- **Wilderness Bound** is the latest released drop listed by Mojang at this check. Its Dappled Forest uses existing sheep, chicken, cow, pig, rabbit and fox populations, with cold variants where applicable. Add biome flavor to their existing gangs rather than inventing a new species for each biome. [26.50 release changelog](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/48826825649933-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-26-50-Changelog-Wilderness-Bound).
- Warm/cold farm variants stay with Hog Wild, The Milk Run and The Pecking Order; wolf variants remain Pack Mentality. Coral Zombie Nautilus remains a Dead Reckoning variant/chapter. Preserve individual appearance and choreography adaptations.
- Dried Ghast, Copper Golem Statue, projectiles, particles and decorative blocks are not automatically recruitable living mob species. Verify the actual entity/life-stage model first.

## The Sift: keep approved designs, track actual availability
Mojang confirms the Sift for **Java & Bedrock in 2027**; Dungeons II and vanilla release coverage must remain separate. The announcement does not promise that every Dungeons II creature will enter vanilla. [September 2026 official recap](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/mclive_sept2026_recap).

The 23 crew and five legendary rows below are the project's previously approved Sift design roster. **Per-creature official naming, actual provider identity and vanilla inclusion remain individually unverified here**, unless subsequently sourced. Preserve provisional labels and semantic archetypes without promoting them to guaranteed vanilla entity IDs. [Dungeons II's official page](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/about-dungeons-ii) confirms the Sift and Blub pet, but is not a complete Java/Bedrock roster.

## Stable identity and naming rights
Preserve one namespaced gang_id per approved crew. Registry/provider entity identity, semantic archetype and visible gang name are separate fields. At the configured Crew Naming Rights threshold, expose the curated alternate as a one-click preset plus server-permitted custom aliases.

Renaming must never split reputation, recruits, alliances, Chapter state, rewards or save identity. Support personal aliases versus shared-world aliases explicitly, permissions and recoverable moderation history, and default/alternate restoration after reload/reconnect.

## Every new mob receives a complete profile
Each row inherits the [full profile standard](Gang-Profile-and-Reference-Standard): individual music track/version/source and cue purpose; dance creator/video/timestamp and rig adaptations; eventual tagging art; personality/quirks; abilities/tells/counterplay; challenge modes; concrete unlocks, recruitable characters and skins; rival-gang control interactions; provider/version status; and performance/runtime evidence.

The recovered hook columns are authored JetSetCraft directions, not assertions about upstream attacks or habitats. For example, Blub Club's aquatic direction remains a project concept until habitat/rig behavior is verified. Do not fill missing music or dance references with made-up links.
