## Read support status precisely
- **Authored:** name, identity or compatibility design exists. This does not prove a rendered rig, completed dance or playable challenge.
- **Wired:** production data/code consumes the record.
- **Provider-tested:** exact provider build booted, live registry reconciled and recorded tests passed.
- **Gameplay-verified:** exact equip/challenge/restore, persistence, multiplayer and visual behavior have their own evidence.

Wave 2B records provider-present and provider-absent verification for Alex's Mobs 1.22.9, Alex's Caves 2.0.2 and Cataclysm 3.31 on Forge 1.20.1. Individual and combined lanes passed nine GameTests and dedicated-server readiness. **That is not full end-user proof for every boss, pet, dance, rig or reward.** Older Wave 2A documents still say provider-present proof is pending; the later Wave 2B checkpoint supersedes that narrow status only. Aether, Twilight, Undergarden and AoA curation must not inherit Wave 2B's test badge.

## Every creature deserves a memorable crew
Each mob needs an individual profile, even when several species share a family. Family membership may share musical grammar and lore; it must not erase that creature's own quirks, references, choreography, silhouette, techniques and rewards. Track missing fields openly. A soundtrack link is not a finished reactive music implementation, and an art brief is not a delivered texture.

### Mob identity and rival control coexist
Gabberina's, Cumbias, Phonkistas, Hardtekkas and other approved major crews can control a mob gang. Keep its stable creature identity and progression while applying the controller's music/dance/boss interpretation. Full takeover removes that mob gang's borrowed ability from the losing rival; partial influence may still qualify for the approved surprise challenge. Hakken Horse remains the explicit Gabberina-controlled horse leader example. Recruitment, challenger unlock screens, skins and naming rights must use existing progression rules and retain unique member identity.

## Compatibility and quality gates
- Keep the same provider-owned entity, UUID, tame/owner data, variants, AI, inventory and boss state. Gear/challenge control is reversible and event-bounded.
- Bosses and multipart helpers need explicit safety treatment; registry presence does not mean safe ordinary spawning. Never count body segments as separate collectible gangs.
- Record actual installed provider versions and entity IDs. Missing IDs become dormant; new safe IDs get generic records until curated. No guessed rebinding by display name.
- Music, dancing, source abilities, arena/biome materials, aquatic/flight/ground routes and tagging rewards must form a coherent encounter. Preserve normal Minecraft play outside events.
- Improve performance and startup alongside content: cached registry/reload discovery, incremental assets and change-driven synchronization. No per-frame full-atlas scans, capability loss or arbitrary roster reductions.
