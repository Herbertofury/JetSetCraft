### Boss-event music assignment — deliberately not general gang radio

- **Target:** Hakken Horse's individual reveal/challenge/boss finale **only if** campaign and horse-gang control make the event eligible. It is **not** added to Gabberina's regular must-add list `GAB-MUSIC-004`–`010`, nor normal horse mob/Bridle Breakers rotation.
- **Motif:** a kinetic, playful horse-themed music/skating encounter with real jumpstyle/gabber/hardtekk character personality and an original, distinct equine-inspired costume. The soundtrack reference makes the horse connection especially vivid, but in-game beat-sync, choreography, exact scene timing, special attacks and scoring have **not** been implemented or approved.
- **Version and rights:** identify the exact Spotify master, verify ISRC/writers/label/audio edition, obtain any required synchronization and distribution clearance or commission a genuinely original alternative. Never download or bundle Spotify audio as an assumed license.
- **Safety:** if the horse gang is reclaimed from Gabberina's, the boss must not use stale ownership data or restored borrowed powers. Existing horses, modded mobs and the player's original world must remain intact; the Hakken Horse human is an event-owned presentation character.
- **Implementation:** [machine-readable owner music registry](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/blob/main/docs/gangs/gabberinas_music.json) and [nonplayable track import metadata](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft/blob/main/src/main/resources/assets/jetsetcraft/jetsetcraft_music/hakken_horse_my_little_white_pony.json). Neither supplies audio or a working boss.
