# Liberty Grind — Helldivers armory and call-in providers

Liberty Grind uses the actual TaCZ Helldivers weapons through an optional adapter and MCDivers as its optional native stratagem provider. The current campaign armory enumerates all 68 live gun definitions in the pinned merged pack, with a selected primary, secondary and armor kit before the pod drops. The call-in catalogue contains 94 researched Helldivers entries plus six JetSetCraft originals; [the complete implementation map](research/LIBERTY_CALLIN_IMPLEMENTATION.md) distinguishes exact native provider calls from functional JetSetCraft adaptations. JetSetCraft still loads without TaCZ. Deployment requires the supported weapons and their magazines to be present in TaCZ's **live server content index**; an installed mod JAR alone is insufficient. The war map explains missing content before the player leaves their world.

## Live campaign armory and the legacy baseline loadout

The campaign reads `TimelessAPI.getAllCommonGunIndex()` as its actual `Set<Map.Entry<ResourceLocation, CommonGunIndex>>`, filters the real `helldiver2` and `zeta` entries, and derives names, ammo, bolt and reload metadata from each live index. The pinned ZIP contains 12 `helldiver2` and 56 `zeta` gun indices. Selected guns are built with the native public builders; provisioning fails atomically if either selection disappears. Resupply includes the two selected guns and an exactly matched loan support weapon in slot 3, tops up owned reserves, and does not change loaded rounds or native heat state. Fuel reloads receive four reserve magazines per selected gun; round-based feeds receive up to three loaded capacities, capped at 256 reserve rounds with overflow-safe arithmetic. Closed-bolt guns have a chambered round; other bolt styles preserve their native chamber semantics.

The pack's seven heat-fed definitions—`zeta:las12`, `zeta:las13`, `zeta:las58`, `zeta:las98`, `zeta:las_16`, `zeta:las_5` and `zeta:quasar`—use the native capacity sentinel `Integer.MAX_VALUE`. JetSetCraft preserves that exact value and their scripts; the armory explains cooling instead of displaying billions of physical rounds. Nineteen definitions carry native heat data, including script-controlled counters. Their initial heat is set to zero through public `IGun.setHeatAmount`; the pinned `GunItemBuilder.build()` does not apply its `setHeatData` flag. The all-gun fixture verifies the real heat fields, exact capacity, matching ammo and bounded reserves for every entry, and the resupply fixture preserves a heated/overheated LAS-12 unchanged. These are direct checks of the [pinned pack release](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/customization/tacz-helldivers-escalation-of-freedom/files/9341504) and published TaCZ JAR, without a replacement weapon definition.

Neither pinned provider ships a Helldivers armor set. The current disclosed light, medium and heavy kits therefore use real vanilla leather, iron and diamond head/chest/leg armor with their actual armor attributes and bounded movement differences. Physical skates remain equipped. These are JetSetCraft field kits, not falsely named native Helldivers armor.

The following starter identities remain the **legacy adapter regression baseline**, with its own six-magazine reserve policy. These IDs were read from the official release ZIP, not inferred from display names. The ZIP contains `data/helldiver2/index/guns/` and `data/zeta/index/ammo/` entries and corresponding gun data.

| Loan | TaCZ resource ID | Magazine resource ID | Loaded capacity | Reserve item stack limit |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| AR-23 Liberator | `helldiver2:ar23_liberator` | `zeta:ar_ammo` | 45 rounds | 20 |
| P-2 Peacemaker | `helldiver2:p2_peacemaker` | `zeta:p113_ammo` | 15 rounds | 8 |

Both definitions use `closed_bolt` and `reload.type: fuel`. In TaCZ's fuel reload implementation, **one reserve ammo item fills a magazine**. The adapter issues six reserve magazine items for each gun, rather than treating each item as one bullet. Each starting gun has one chambered round plus capacity minus one in its magazine. Resupply tops carried loan magazines up to six and leaves chamber/magazine state alone, so players still use TaCZ's reload controls. [TaCZ reload implementation](https://github.com/MCModderAnchor/TACZ/blob/1.20.1/src/main/java/com/tacz/guns/item/ModernKineticGunItem.java).

The original standalone pack uses the `helldiver2` namespace too, so detecting a namespace is not enough. The adapter checks both exact guns, their expected merged-pack ammo IDs, loaded ammo indices, bounded magazine capacity, closed-bolt operation and fuel reload semantics. Missing or incompatible data blocks deployment with an actionable explanation.

## Integration contract

`compat/LibertyTaczCompat.java` retains the baseline admission/loan utilities and legacy loadout contract:

- `available()` and `status()` for admission and the war-map message.
- `provision(ServerPlayer)` for a fully staged loadout in inventory slots **0, 1, 9 and 10**. Slot 2 is reserved for the mission spray can. Provisioning refuses occupied target slots.
- `resupply(ServerPlayer)` to top up owned reserve magazines, without manufacturing another pair of guns or overwriting other inventory items.
- `markLoan`, `isLoan(stack)` and `isLoan(stack, owner)` for the manager's inventory and dropped-item cleanup.

Loan NBT uses `jetsetcraft_liberty_loan` and `jetsetcraft_liberty_loan_owner`. The mission manager owns active-session validation, resupply cooldowns, inventory escrow, prevention of item export, and cleanup on extraction, failure, disconnect and recovery. An owner marker does not replace session ownership or a saved return snapshot.

The adapter reflects only public TaCZ methods: `TimelessAPI.getCommonGunIndex/getCommonAmmoIndex`, `CommonGunIndex.getGunData`, gun-data accessors, `GunItemBuilder`, `AmmoItemBuilder`, and `IAmmo.getAmmoId`. Reflection metadata is cached; content availability is rechecked against the live indices so pack reloads cannot leave a stale successful admission result. There are no TaCZ imports, registry replacements or mandatory TaCZ dependencies. The campaign adds a narrowly scoped optional native projectile Mixin for ownership/terrain containment, described below. Failed reflection disables the affected optional path; it never invents a replacement selected armory weapon. [Public API](https://github.com/MCModderAnchor/TACZ/blob/1.20.1/src/main/java/com/tacz/guns/api/TimelessAPI.java), [gun builder](https://github.com/MCModderAnchor/TACZ/blob/1.20.1/src/main/java/com/tacz/guns/api/item/builder/GunItemBuilder.java), [ammo builder](https://github.com/MCModderAnchor/TACZ/blob/1.20.1/src/main/java/com/tacz/guns/api/item/builder/AmmoItemBuilder.java).

## Native stratagem integration and home containment

`LibertyHelldiversCompat` admits exact MCDivers **1.0.3** and creates real provider beacons through public `StratagemBeaconEntity.planted(Level, Stratagem, Vec3, Vec3, Entity)`. The engine marks the unspawned beacon before adding it, then adopts native ordnance, Eagle, laser and sentry actors only after checking their actual registry/class, owner, accepted call and active operation. Native calls are used only in dedicated districts. Home defense uses the corresponding bounded JetSetCraft effects. The field panel reports the backend that is actually available.

MCDivers' JAR metadata requires Forge `[47.4.22,)` and Minecraft `[1.20.1,1.21)` with no other mandatory companion mod. The selected JetSetCraft Forge pin is newer than that minimum. The provider's public project link in the JAR did not expose a usable public source tree during inspection, so the exact published class signatures and bytecode are the integration evidence; the bridge does not assume an unrelated source branch matches this binary.

The bridge reads exact-version private MCDivers `owner` / `ownerId` fields where no public owner accessor exists. It does not write those ownership fields. HIGHEST-priority native explosion filtering removes affected blocks and foreign entities before MCDivers' own explosion listener can apply damage. Direct native damage is also restricted to exact operation enemies. Sentry light cleanup restores recorded empty positions only while they still contain `mcdivers:sentry_light`, preserving later unrelated block changes.

TaCZ's custom bullet sweep bypasses Forge's ordinary projectile-impact event. It also calls `ITargetEntity.onProjectileHit` before its cancelable gun-damage event, and applies ignition before normal living-damage cancellation. `LibertyTaczProjectileMixin` is therefore an optional `@Pseudo` target with no TaCZ imports. It guards entity hits at their first instruction and intercepts block hits before projectile-reactive block callbacks or fire placement. The exact version, expected native hit methods, injectable marker interface and terrain fields are checked before safe admission. An absent or mismatched provider remains optional; unsupported owned bullets are culled rather than allowed to affect the ordinary world.

Only an admitted owned bullet has its private `igniteBlock` and `explosionDestroyBlock` flags disabled. No global config changes. Owned explosive block impacts still use the actual public `ExplodeUtil.createExplosion` and native damage pipeline, with destruction disabled and affected entities filtered by operation ownership. The guard also protects nonliving practice targets and existing interactive blocks. Ordinary TaCZ gunfire outside Liberty operations keeps its normal provider behavior.

Vanilla mission projectiles use Forge's ordinary impact hook. Owned block impacts stop before reactive block callbacks, including redstone targets. Owned Witch potions retain their actual potion data and source entity, but a bounded splash adapter filters every effect, healing and water interaction by exact operation membership before application. It preserves the four-block falloff without creating an unfiltered lingering cloud, touching fire/campfires or healing unrelated residents. Ordinary potions outside Liberty retain vanilla behavior. This separate boundary is necessary because potion effects and healing do not necessarily produce a living-damage event.

Home defense also gates deployment on two narrow Minecraft 1.20.1 contact guards. They keep mission participants and exact owned actors from invoking pressure-plate scans, inside-block callbacks, ore/egg stepping and terrain-changing fall callbacks. Ordinary movement and fall damage remain active; the guards release when the operation ends. Exact development/production method aliases and injection counts are checked, so an unapplied contact boundary cannot silently enable home deployment. Mission mob initialization suppresses native foreign jockeys/raid membership; native reinforcement and conversion hooks prevent unowned children. Home-only species substitutions avoid Silverfish block emergence and Skeleton's unhooked Stray conversion.
